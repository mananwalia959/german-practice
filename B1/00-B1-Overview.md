# B1 — Selbstständige Sprachverwendung
## B1 — Independent Language Use

---

## CEFR B1 Level Description

At the B1 level (Threshold), you can:

- Understand the main points of clear standard speech on familiar topics encountered in work, school, and leisure
- Deal with most situations likely to arise while travelling in a German-speaking region
- Produce simple connected text on topics that are familiar or of personal interest
- Describe experiences and events, dreams, hopes, and ambitions
- Briefly give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans
- Understand the main points of radio and TV programmes on current affairs or topics of personal interest when the delivery is relatively slow and clear

**In grammar terms**, B1 marks the transition from basic to independent usage. You will master all German tenses (including Plusquamperfekt and Futur I), the passive voice in all tenses, Konjunktiv I and II, relative clauses in all cases, and complex sentence structures. This level builds a solid grammatical foundation for academic and professional German.

---

## Voraussetzungen / Prerequisites

**A2 completed.** Before starting B1, you should be confident with:

- All four German cases (Nominativ, Akkusativ, Dativ, Genitiv basics)
- Present tense (Präsens) including irregular verbs
- Simple past (Präteritum) of sein, haben, and modal verbs
- Perfect tense (Perfekt) with haben and sein
- Basic adjective declension with definite and indefinite articles
- Subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn, als, ob
- Comparative and superlative forms
- Reflexive verbs and pronouns
- Basic prepositions with Akkusativ, Dativ, and Wechselpräpositionen
- Nebensatz word order (verb-final)

If any of these areas feel weak, review [A2](../A2/00-A2-Overview.md) before continuing.

## Lernplan / Study Plan

### Empfohlener Zeitplan / Recommended Schedule

**Gesamtdauer: 9–12 Wochen** (bei 5–6 Stunden pro Woche)

| Woche | Lektionen | Schwerpunkt | Hinweise |
|-------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | 01–02 | Plusquamperfekt, Futur I | Complete the German tense system |
| 2 | 03 | Konjunktiv II alle Formen | Take extra time — Konjunktiv II is essential for B1 |
| 3 | 04 | Passiv alle Zeitformen | Practice transforming active to passive across all tenses |
| 4 | 05–06 | Relativsätze | All relative clause types; practice combining sentences |
| 5 | 07–08 | n-Deklination, Adjektivdeklination | Focus on pattern recognition and drilling |
| 6 | 09–10 | Genitiv, Präpositionen mit Genitiv | The final case mastery — read German texts to see Genitiv in context |
| 7 | 11–12 | Doppelkonnektoren, Indirekte Rede | Complex connectors and reported speech |
| 8 | 13–14 | Partizipiale Adjektive, Nominalisierung | Bridging toward formal/written German style |
| 9 | 15–16 | Es als Platzhalter, Komplexe Satzstrukturen | Sentence-level mastery and fluency building |
| 10 | 17 | Temporalsätze und Kausalsätze | Consolidate all clause types |
| 11–12 | 18 | Zusammenfassung und Prüfung | Full review, practice exam, identify weak spots |

### Tägliche Routine / Daily Routine

1. **Grammatik (30 min)** — Work through the current lesson section
2. **Wortschatz (15 min)** — Learn 5–8 new words from the vocabulary lists
3. **Übungen (30 min)** — Complete exercises; check answers only after finishing
4. **Lesen/Hören (15 min)** — Read or listen to authentic B1-level German material
5. **Wiederholung (15 min)** — Review previous lesson notes and difficult points

### Lerntipps / Study Tips

- **Merke!** At B1, grammar and vocabulary must work together. Always learn new grammar with full example sentences, not isolated rules.
- **Tipp:** Start reading short German news articles (Deutsche Welle, Nachrichtenleicht) alongside the lessons from Week 3 onward.
- **Tipp:** Keep a dedicated error notebook (Fehlerheft). Write down every mistake from exercises and review it weekly.
- **Achtung!** Do not skip Konjunktiv II (Lesson 03) or Passiv (Lesson 04) — they are tested heavily on the Goethe B1 exam.

## Goethe-Zertifikat B1 — Prüfungsvorbereitung / Exam Preparation

### Prüfungsformat / Exam Format

The Goethe-Zertifikat B1 consists of four modules, each graded independently:

| Modul | Dauer | Punkte | Beschreibung |
|-------|-------|--------|--------------|
| **Lesen** (Reading) | 65 min | 100 | 5 parts: matching, multiple choice, true/false, forum posts, instructions |
| **Hören** (Listening) | 40 min | 100 | 4 parts: everyday conversations, radio segments, discussions |
| **Schreiben** (Writing) | 60 min | 100 | 3 parts: email/letter, forum comment, formal message |
| **Sprechen** (Speaking) | 15 min (pair) | 100 | 3 parts: planning together, presentation, discussion |

**Bestanden / Pass:** 60 points per module (60%)

### Grammatik im Fokus der Prüfung / Grammar Focus for the Exam

The following B1 grammar topics appear most frequently on the Goethe B1 exam:

| Priorität | Thema | Prüfungsteil | Lektion |
|-----------|-------|--------------|---------|
| Hoch | Konjunktiv II (Wünsche, irreale Bedingungen) | Schreiben, Sprechen | 03 |
| Hoch | Passiv (Vorgangsbeschreibungen) | Lesen, Schreiben | 04 |
| Hoch | Relativsätze (komplexe Sätze bilden) | Schreiben, Sprechen | 05, 06 |
| Hoch | Konnektoren (Argumentation aufbauen) | Schreiben, Lesen | 11, 17 |
| Mittel | Indirekte Rede (Berichte verstehen) | Lesen, Hören | 12 |
| Mittel | Genitiv und Genitivpräpositionen | Lesen | 09, 10 |
| Mittel | Plusquamperfekt (Erzählungen verstehen) | Lesen, Hören | 01 |
| Mittel | Nominalisierung (formelle Texte) | Lesen | 14 |

### Tipps für die Prüfung / Exam Tips

**Lesen (Reading)**
- Practice reading for gist first, then for detail — do not get stuck on unknown words
- Pay special attention to negation and Konjunktiv II in the true/false sections
- Time management is critical: allocate roughly 12 minutes per part

**Hören (Listening)**
- Listen to Deutsche Welle "Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten" daily in the weeks before the exam
- In Part 1, read the questions before the audio plays
- Watch for distractors — the audio often mentions an option before correcting it

**Schreiben (Writing)**
- Use a variety of Konnektoren (nicht nur "und", "aber", "weil")
- For the formal message: use Konjunktiv II for polite requests (Ich hätte gerne..., Könnten Sie...)
- Plan your text structure before writing — introduction, body, closing
- Aim for 80–120 words per writing task; quality over quantity

**Sprechen (Speaking)**
- In the planning task, use phrases like: Ich schlage vor..., Wie wäre es, wenn..., Einverstanden?
- For the presentation, prepare a clear structure: Einleitung, Situation in meinem Heimatland, Vorteile/Nachteile, meine Meinung
- In the discussion, react to your partner: Da stimme ich zu / Das sehe ich anders, weil...

### Nützliche Redemittel für die Prüfung / Useful Phrases for the Exam

**Meinung ausdrücken / Expressing opinions:**
- Meiner Meinung nach... / Ich bin der Meinung, dass...
- Ich finde es wichtig/richtig/falsch, dass...
- Aus meiner Sicht / Aus meiner Erfahrung...

**Argumentieren / Arguing:**
- Einerseits... andererseits...
- Ein Vorteil/Nachteil ist, dass...
- Dafür/Dagegen spricht, dass...
- Außerdem / Darüber hinaus / Zusätzlich...

**Vorschläge machen / Making suggestions:**
- Ich würde vorschlagen, dass...
- Wie wäre es mit + Dativ?
- Wir könnten... / Wir sollten...
