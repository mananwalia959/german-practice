# Deutsche Grammatik: Intensivkurs A1–B2
## German Grammar: Intensive Course A1–B2

A comprehensive, self-contained German grammar learning system covering CEFR levels A1 through B2. Designed for intensive self-study with complete grammar explanations, examples, exercises, and answer keys.

---

## Struktur / Structure

| Niveau | Lektionen | Verben | Nomen | Beschreibung |
|--------|-----------|--------|-------|--------------|
| [A1](A1/00-A1-Overview.md) | 20 | 100 | 150 | Beginner — basic communication, present tense, cases intro |
| [A2](A2/00-A2-Overview.md) | 19 | 100 | 130 | Elementary — past tenses, adjective declension, subordinate clauses |
| [B1](B1/00-B1-Overview.md) | 18 | 100 | 120 | Intermediate — all tenses, passive, Konjunktiv, complex sentences |
| [B2](B2/00-B2-Overview.md) | 18 | 100 | 120 | Upper Intermediate — academic German, stylistics, advanced structures |

**Total: 75 grammar lessons + 400 verbs + 520 nouns**

---

## Wie benutzt man diesen Kurs / How to Use

1. Start with level A1 if you are a beginner, or choose your current level
2. Open the **Overview** file (`00-XX-Overview.md`) for your level
3. Follow lessons in order — each builds on the previous
4. For each lesson:
   - Read the grammar explanation carefully
   - Study all example sentences
   - Complete exercises **without** looking at answers
   - Check your answers and review mistakes
   - Study related vocabulary from `vocabulary/` folder
5. Complete the final review lesson before moving to the next level

## Legende / Legend

Throughout the lessons you will see:

- **Merke!** — Important rule to remember
- **Achtung!** — Common mistake or exception to watch out for
- **Tipp** — Helpful learning tip
- Exercises marked with difficulty: (A) = easy, (B) = medium, (C) = challenging
