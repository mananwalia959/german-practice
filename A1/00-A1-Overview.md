# A1 - Deutsche Grammatik: Grundstufe

## CEFR A1 Level Description

Upon completing the A1 level, learners can:

- Understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases aimed at satisfying concrete needs.
- Introduce themselves and others, ask and answer questions about personal details such as where they live, people they know, and things they have.
- Interact in a simple way provided the other person talks slowly and clearly and is prepared to help.
- Read and understand short, simple texts such as signs, notices, and forms.
- Write short, simple messages (postcards, emails) and fill in personal details on forms.

**Target vocabulary:** ~500 active words | ~800 passive words

---

## Prerequisites

**None.** This course begins from absolute zero. No prior knowledge of German is required.

You should be comfortable reading Latin script (the standard Western alphabet). All grammatical terminology is explained as it is introduced.

## Study Plan (8 Weeks)

The recommended pace is **2-3 lessons per week**, completing the entire A1 level in approximately 8 weeks. Each lesson requires roughly 45-60 minutes of focused study, plus 15-20 minutes of daily vocabulary review.

### Weekly Schedule

| Woche | Lektionen | Schwerpunkt (Focus) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **1** | 01, 02 | Sounds of German; the two most important verbs (*sein*, *haben*) |
| **2** | 03, 04 | Present tense conjugation for regular and irregular verbs |
| **3** | 05, 06, 07 | Nominative case, articles, pronouns, and negation |
| **4** | 08, 09 | Question formation and German word order rules |
| **5** | 10, 11, 12 | Accusative case, possessives, and modal verbs |
| **6** | 13, 14 | Separable verbs and imperative commands |
| **7** | 15, 16, 17 | Prepositions (accusative and dative) and plural formation |
| **8** | 18, 19, 20 | Adjectives, numbers, conjunctions, final review and practice exam |

### Daily Routine (recommended)

1. **Vocabulary review** (15 min) — Review flashcards from vocabulary files; add new words from the current lesson.
2. **Lesson study** (45-60 min, 2-3 days/week) — Read the lesson, study examples, complete exercises.
3. **Active practice** (10 min) — Write 5 sentences using the day's grammar point, then read them aloud.

## Study Method Guide

### How to Use Each Lesson

Every lesson follows a consistent structure. Here is how to work through them effectively:

1. **Read the explanation (Erklärung)** — Understand the rule before attempting examples. Each grammar point includes English explanations alongside German terminology.
2. **Study the examples (Beispiele)** — Do not just read them. Cover the translation and try to understand the German first. Then check.
3. **Learn the tables (Tabellen)** — Grammar tables (conjugation, declension) must be memorized. Write them out by hand at least three times.
4. **Complete the exercises (Übungen)** — Do every exercise in writing. Check your answers. Repeat any section where you scored below 80%.
5. **Review previous lessons** — Before starting a new lesson, spend 10 minutes reviewing the key tables from the previous one.

### Memorization Techniques

- **Spaced repetition:** Review material at increasing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days).
- **Write by hand:** Handwriting activates deeper memory encoding than typing.
- **Speak aloud:** German pronunciation must be practiced from Lesson 01 onward. Read every example sentence out loud.
- **Create example sentences:** For each grammar rule, write 3 original sentences from your own life.
- **Color coding:** Use one color for masculine (der), another for feminine (die), and a third for neuter (das) when writing vocabulary.

### Common Mistakes to Watch For

- Forgetting the article when learning nouns.
- Applying English word order to German sentences.
- Confusing *nicht* (negating verbs/adjectives) with *kein* (negating nouns).
- Neglecting pronunciation practice in early lessons.

## Goethe A1 Exam Preparation (Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1)

### Exam Structure

The Goethe-Zertifikat A1 consists of four parts:

| Prüfungsteil (Section) | Dauer (Duration) | Punkte (Points) |
|-------------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Lesen** (Reading) | 25 minutes | 25 |
| **Hören** (Listening) | ~20 minutes | 25 |
| **Schreiben** (Writing) | 20 minutes | 25 |
| **Sprechen** (Speaking) | ~15 minutes | 25 |

**Passing score:** 60 out of 100 total points (60%). You must pass each section individually.

### Section Details and Tips

#### Lesen (Reading)
- **Part 1:** Match short texts to situations (signs, notices, ads).
- **Part 2:** Read short messages and answer true/false questions.
- **Tip:** Focus on keywords. You do not need to understand every word — identify the main information.

#### Hören (Listening)
- **Part 1:** Listen to short announcements and match them.
- **Part 2:** Listen to short conversations and answer questions.
- **Tip:** Practise with German audio daily. Podcasts such as *Slow German* or *Deutsche Welle A1* are excellent resources.

#### Schreiben (Writing)
- Write a short message (email, postcard, or form) of approximately 30 words.
- **Tip:** Practise writing short messages covering: greeting, reason for writing, one piece of information, closing. Lessons 08 and 09 (questions and word order) are especially relevant here.

#### Sprechen (Speaking)
- **Part 1:** Introduce yourself (name, age, country, language, job/study, hobbies).
- **Part 2:** Ask for and provide everyday information using prompt cards.
- **Part 3:** Make simple requests and respond to them.
- **Tip:** Prepare your self-introduction thoroughly. Practise the question forms from Lesson 08 and the vocabulary from the 150-Nomen list.

### Mapping Lessons to Exam Sections

| Exam Section | Most Relevant Lessons |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Lesen | 05, 07, 09, 17, 18 |
| Hören | 01, 03, 04, 08, 12 |
| Schreiben | 02, 03, 08, 09, 19 |
| Sprechen | 01, 02, 06, 08, 14 |

### Recommended Exam Preparation Timeline

- **Weeks 1-6:** Complete lessons 01-17 at the normal study pace.
- **Week 7:** Finish lessons 18-20. Begin timed practice with sample exams.
- **Week 8:** Full practice exams under timed conditions. Review weak areas.
- **Final days:** Review vocabulary lists, practise speaking introduction, and rest before the exam.
