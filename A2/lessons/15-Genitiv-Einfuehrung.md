# Lektion 15: Genitiv — Einführung
## Genitive Case — Introduction

**Niveau:** A2
**Lernziele:**
- Form the genitive case for all article types
- Use genitive to express possession
- Understand the -s/-es ending on masculine/neuter nouns

---

## 1. Einführung

> Das ist **das Auto meines Vaters**. (That's my father's car.)
> Ich kenne den Namen **des Restaurants** nicht. (I don't know the name of the restaurant.)
> Trotz **des Regens** gehen wir spazieren. (Despite the rain, we go for a walk.)

The genitive case shows **possession or belonging**. It answers the question: **Wessen?** (Whose?)

---

## 2. Grammatik-Erklärung

### 2.1 Genitive Articles

| | Maskulin | Feminin | Neutrum | Plural |
|-|---------|---------|---------|--------|
| **Definit** | des | der | des | der |
| **Indefinit** | eines | einer | eines | — |
| **Negativ** | keines | keiner | keines | keiner |
| **Possessiv** | meines | meiner | meines | meiner |

### 2.2 Noun Endings in Genitive

**Masculine and Neuter** nouns add **-s** or **-es**:
- One syllable → prefer **-es**: des Mann**es**, des Haus**es**, des Kind**es**, des Tag**es**
- Multiple syllables → prefer **-s**: des Vater**s**, des Lehrers, des Computers, des Auto**s**
- Nouns ending in **-s, -ß, -z, -x, -sch** → always **-es**: des Hals**es**, des Fuß**es**

**Feminine and Plural** nouns: **no ending** added to the noun itself.
- der Name der Frau (no -s added to Frau)
- die Farbe der Häuser (no -s added to Häuser)

### 2.3 Usage: Possession and Belonging

| English | German Genitive |
|---------|----------------|
| the man's car | das Auto **des Mannes** |
| the teacher's book | das Buch **des Lehrers** |
| the woman's bag | die Tasche **der Frau** |
| the child's name | der Name **des Kindes** |
| the end of the film | das Ende **des Films** |
| the color of the house | die Farbe **des Hauses** |

The genitive noun phrase usually comes **after** the noun it modifies.

### 2.4 Proper Names: -s without apostrophe

In German, proper names add **-s** directly (no apostrophe!):
- Peter**s** Buch (Peter's book)
- Anna**s** Wohnung (Anna's apartment)
- Berlin**s** Museen (Berlin's museums)

**Exception:** Names ending in **-s, -z, -x** → add apostrophe only:
- Thomas**'** Buch (Thomas' book)
- Klaus**'** Auto

### 2.5 Alternative: von + Dativ

In spoken German, **von + Dativ** is very common instead of genitive:
- das Auto **des Mannes** = das Auto **von dem Mann** (= vom Mann)
- der Name **der Firma** = der Name **von der Firma**

> **Tipp:** In everyday speech, von + Dativ is perfectly fine. Genitive is more formal/written.

### 2.6 Adjective Endings in Genitive

After definite article: **-en**
- das Auto des alt**en** Mannes
- die Tasche der klein**en** Frau

After indefinite article: **-en**
- das Buch eines gut**en** Lehrers
- das Ende eines lang**en** Films

## 6. Zusammenfassung

- Genitive answers **Wessen?** (Whose?)
- Definite articles: **des / der / des / der**
- Masculine + Neuter nouns add **-s** or **-es** in genitive
- Feminine + Plural nouns: **no ending** on the noun
- Proper names: add **-s** (no apostrophe): Peters Buch
- Colloquial alternative: **von + Dativ** (das Auto von meinem Vater)
- Adjective endings in genitive: always **-en** after articles
