# A2 — Elementare Sprachverwendung
## A2 — Elementary Language Use

**CEFR Level A2**: You can understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of most immediate relevance (e.g., personal and family information, shopping, local geography, employment). You can communicate in simple, routine tasks requiring a direct exchange of information on familiar matters. You can describe in simple terms aspects of your background, immediate environment, and matters in areas of immediate need.

By the end of A2 you will be able to:
- Talk about past events using Perfekt and Präteritum
- Describe people and things with declined adjectives
- Compare things using Komparativ and Superlativ
- Build complex sentences with subordinate clauses (weil, dass, wenn, ob, als, etc.)
- Use relative clauses to add detail
- Express wishes and polite requests with Konjunktiv II
- Understand and form basic passive sentences
- Use the Genitiv case in common expressions
- Handle verbs with fixed prepositions
- Form indirect questions and infinitive constructions with *zu*

---

## Voraussetzungen / Prerequisites

**A1 completed.** Before starting A2, you should be confident with:
- Present tense of regular and irregular verbs
- Separable and inseparable prefix verbs
- Modal verbs in present tense
- Nominativ, Akkusativ, and Dativ cases (basic usage)
- Basic sentence structure (Satzklammer)
- Common prepositions with Akkusativ and Dativ
- Numbers, time, and basic question words
- Vocabulary: ~100 verbs, ~150 nouns from A1

If any of these feel shaky, review [A1](../A1/00-A1-Overview.md) before continuing.

## Lernplan / Study Plan

### Recommended Pace: 10–12 weeks

| Woche | Lektionen | Schwerpunkt |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | 1–2 | **Perfekt** — Learn to talk about past events with regular and irregular verbs |
| 2 | 3–4 | **Präteritum & Dativ** — Past tense of key verbs; deepen Dativ understanding |
| 3 | 5–6 | **Reflexive Verben & Wechselpräpositionen** — Reflexive actions; two-way prepositions |
| 4 | 7–8 | **Adjektivdeklination** — Adjective endings in all cases and without articles |
| 5 | 9 | **Komparativ & Superlativ** — Comparing things; review adjective declension |
| 6 | 10–11 | **Nebensätze** — Subordinate clauses with all major conjunctions |
| 7 | 12 | **Relativsätze** — Relative clauses; review all subordinate clause word order |
| 8 | 13–14 | **Konjunktiv II & Passiv** — Wishes, polite forms, and passive voice basics |
| 9 | 15–16 | **Genitiv & Verben mit Präpositionen** — Genitive case; fixed verb-preposition pairs |
| 10 | 17–18 | **Indirekte Fragen & Infinitiv mit zu** — Indirect questions; infinitive constructions |
| 11–12 | 19 | **Zusammenfassung & Prüfung** — Full review, practice exam, and self-assessment |

**Daily routine (recommended):**
- 30–45 min grammar lesson (read, understand, take notes)
- 15–20 min exercises (complete without checking answers first)
- 10–15 min vocabulary review (use flashcards or cover-and-recall)
- 10 min review previous lesson's weak points

**Tipp:** After each lesson, try to write 5–10 sentences using the new grammar in a personal context (your daily life, your plans, your past). This active production accelerates learning far more than passive review.

## Goethe-Zertifikat A2 — Prüfungsvorbereitung / Exam Preparation

The **Goethe-Zertifikat A2** (also known as *Goethe-Zertifikat A2: Fit in Deutsch* for younger learners) tests all four skills. Here is how this course maps to the exam.

### Exam Structure

| Prüfungsteil | Dauer | Punkte | Beschreibung |
|-------------|-------|--------|--------------|
| Lesen (Reading) | 30 min | 45 | 4 parts — short texts, notices, ads, emails |
| Hören (Listening) | 30 min | 45 | 4 parts — announcements, conversations, interviews |
| Schreiben (Writing) | 30 min | 45 | 2 parts — fill-in message/form + short personal email |
| Sprechen (Speaking) | 15 min | 45 | 3 parts — introduce yourself, discuss everyday topic, make requests |

**Passing score:** 60% overall (108 / 180 points), with at least 45% in each section.

### How This Course Prepares You

- **Lesen / Schreiben:** Lessons 10–12 (subordinate and relative clauses) and Lessons 7–9 (adjective declension, comparisons) give you the grammar needed to understand and write typical A2-level texts.
- **Hören:** Lessons 1–3 (Perfekt and Präteritum) are critical — listening passages frequently use past tenses. Practice reading example sentences aloud.
- **Schreiben:** Lesson 17 (indirect questions) and Lesson 13 (Konjunktiv II for polite requests) are directly tested in the writing section — emails, invitations, and polite responses.
- **Sprechen:** Lesson 5 (reflexive verbs for daily routines), Lesson 9 (comparisons), and Lesson 13 (polite forms) cover the most common speaking tasks.

### Exam Tips

- **Merke!** The exam expects A2-level grammar, not perfection. Simple, correct sentences score higher than ambitious, error-filled ones.
- **Achtung!** Time management is key. In the reading section, read the questions first, then scan the texts.
- **Tipp:** For the writing section, learn standard email phrases: *Liebe/r ...*, *Vielen Dank für ...*, *Könnten Sie mir bitte ...*, *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*.
- Practice with official Goethe-Institut sample exams (available free at [goethe.de](https://www.goethe.de)).
- Use Lesson 19 (A2 Zusammenfassung und Prüfung) as your final self-assessment before taking the exam.
